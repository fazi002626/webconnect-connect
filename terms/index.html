1. The service

WebConnect shows your store's orders and products in the WebConnect app and, if you choose, sends order confirmation and delivery messages to your own customers from your own WhatsApp number.

2. Your responsibilities

  • You are the controller of your customers' personal data; we process it only on your instructions, to provide the service.
  • You make sure you may contact your customers about their orders (for example on WhatsApp) under the laws that apply to you.
  • You keep your WebConnect login safe and tell us about any misuse.

3. How we process your customers' data (data processing terms)

  • Purpose: showing your orders to you and sending the order messages you switch on. Nothing else.
  • Data: customer name, phone, email and shipping address on orders; order items and amounts.
  • Confidentiality: only the operator of WebConnect can access the systems; access is protected by strong passwords and two-step verification.
  • Security: data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest (Google Firebase); store access tokens are additionally encrypted (AES-256-GCM).
  • Sub-processors: Google Firebase / Google Cloud (storage), Render (server), Contabo (messaging server), Meta WhatsApp (message delivery from your own linked number).
  • Retention: we keep data while your store is connected. When you uninstall, store access is removed at once and customers' personal details are erased within 48 hours of Shopify's shop redaction request.
  • Customer rights: we act on customer data and deletion requests forwarded by Shopify or by you within 30 days.
  • Incidents: we tell you without undue delay, and within 72 hours, if a security incident affects your customers' data (see our Incident Response Policy).
  • No selling: we never sell personal data or use it for advertising.
