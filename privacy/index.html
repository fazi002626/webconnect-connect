WebConnect — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

WebConnect ("the app") helps online shop owners confirm orders and track deliveries. This policy explains what data the app uses when a merchant connects a Shopify store, and how it is handled.

Data we access

  • Orders (read only): order number, date, items, amounts, payment and fulfillment status.
  • Customer details on those orders: name, phone number, email and shipping address — used only so the merchant can confirm the order with their own customer and arrange delivery.

We request only the read_orders and read_customers permissions. We never change orders, products or payments in the store.

How we use it

  • To show the merchant's orders inside their WebConnect account.
  • To let the merchant send order confirmation and delivery update messages to their own customers.

We do not sell data, show ads, or share data with third parties other than the infrastructure that runs the service (Google Firebase for storage, Render for the server).

Storage and security

  • Shopify access tokens are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) on our server and are never shown in the app.
  • Order data is stored in the merchant's own WebConnect account and is visible only to that merchant and team members they invite.

Deleting data

  • Uninstalling WebConnect from Shopify deletes the store's access tokens immediately.
  • We respond to Shopify's privacy requests (customer data request, customer redaction and shop redaction) within 30 days.
  • Merchants can ask us to delete their WebConnect data at any time by emailing us.
