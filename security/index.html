WebConnect — Security and Incident Response Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Everyday safeguards

  • Access to production systems (Firebase, servers, Shopify Partner account) is limited to the WebConnect operator and protected by strong, unique passwords and two-step verification.
  • Servers are reached only with SSH keys; secrets live in environment files readable only by the service.
  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest; Shopify access tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM.
  • Access to personal data in Google Cloud is recorded in audit logs.
  • Test stores and test data are kept separate from merchants' live data.
  • Data is stored with Google Firebase, which keeps redundant, encrypted copies, so a single failure does not lose data.

When an incident happens

  1. Detect and contain (same day): revoke exposed keys or tokens, rotate passwords, stop affected services.
  2. Assess (within 24 hours): find out what happened, which merchants and which data are affected, using audit and server logs.
  3. Notify (within 72 hours): tell affected merchants by email — what happened, what data, what we did and what they should do. Notify Shopify and authorities where the law requires.
  4. Fix and learn: remove the cause, restore service, and write down what changes prevent it from happening again.
